## Delivered behavior

- The session router directs future sessions to balanced parallel production:
  the smallest effective team, independent ready work, exact file ownership,
  one integrator and one Studio operator.
- User-requested worker chats carry task boundaries, dependencies, checks and
  handback conditions. Starting team shapes are guidance rather than quotas;
  concurrency decreases when workers wait, overlap or exceed integration capacity.
- Modification and debugging contracts retain exact identity, failure evidence,
  discriminating controls, owning-layer corrections and original-case replay.
  Visual debugging qualifies capture paths and subjects before interpreting pixels.
- The task template records decision source, allowed current effects and deferred
  activation when approvals differ. A record neither grants nor authenticates authority.
- Durable rationale lives in [DECISIONS.md](DECISIONS.md); reusable contracts and
  the case format live in the [engine guide](../docs/roblox-workflow-engines.md)
  and [debug template](../templates/ROBLOX-DEBUG-CASE.template.md).
